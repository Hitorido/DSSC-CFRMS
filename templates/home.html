About the reservation trend

Each point is the number of reservations scheduled in that calendar month. The chart covers the current month and the five preceding months.

  • Reservations are grouped by their requested facility-use date, not the date the request was submitted.
  • Every reservation counts once, regardless of whether it is pending, approved, completed, declined, or cancelled.
  • Use month-to-month changes to spot shifts in campus space usage.

About reservation status

Each slice shows how many reservations are currently in a workflow status. This panel lists the exact counts so you can read the values without hovering over the chart.

  • Pending: waiting for a reviewer decision.
  • Approved: accepted and scheduled.
  • Completed: the approved booking has taken place.
  • Declined: a reviewer did not approve the request.
  • Cancelled: the booking was withdrawn.

About most requested facilities

Each bar counts reservations for one facility. The chart shows up to five facilities with the most requests during the current month and the five preceding months.

  • Reservations are grouped by the facility requested and scheduled-use date.
  • Bars are sorted from the highest request count to the lowest.
  • Open this panel to read the exact count for each facility; counts represent reservation requests, not hours or capacity.
